          Platform Engineering
              
           Internal Developer Platform(IDP)

Platform Engineering is the practice of building an internal developer platform(IDP) that makes it easy for developers to:
Build application
Deploy services
Manage infrastructure
Use cloud resources
Follow company standards
Without manually configuring everything themselves.

In simple term Think of Platform Engineering as:
   “ Building a self-service system for developers.”
Instead of every developer:
Configuring server 
Setting up kubernets
Writing deployement scripts 
Handling CI/CD manually 
The platform team builds a reuasable platform that automates all of it.



What is IDP?
- is the actual platform/tool developers use.
It provides:
Self-service deployment
Templates
CI/CD pipelines
Kubernet environments
Monitoring 
Secrets management 
Logging
